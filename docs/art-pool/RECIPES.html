SIX TRADES, SIXTY SHOTS

Same method that locked auto. For each trade: make shot 01 first with the full style paragraph, keep generating until it's right, then feed 01 as the reference image for shots 02–10 so the whole trade matches. Four heroes and six bands per trade; nine keepers is plenty, seven is fine. Save each keeper as the filename on its card, drop them in the folder named on the trade, drop the manifest beside them, and tell me the trade is in — I curate, then one call puts it in the pool.

The four laws for every shot
  • No words anywhere. No signs, plates, labels, screens with text, brand marks. If Grok sneaks lettering in, regenerate — don't crop.
  • No people. No hands, no faces, no silhouettes. Tools and light do the talking.
  • Nothing that could be mistaken for the shop's own place. Unmarked trucks, plain vans, generic buildings, no house numbers.
  • Portrait 9:16, every time, edge to edge. The split hero uses portraits as-is for desktop and phone; nothing needs resizing. If a shot comes back with blurry bars top and bottom, it is not a portrait — toss it and regenerate. (Lesson from the contractor set: the words "wide banner crop" and "top and bottom can be plain" in the old band prompts made Grok paint the bars itself. The band prompts now say the opposite.)

Check heroes for the auto-shot lesson: wheels and geometry have to be physically possible. If a perspective looks wrong to you, it is wrong.
